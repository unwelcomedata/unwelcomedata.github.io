@unwelcomedata

Data on things people have opinions about.

Charts and datasets built from public data. Some thought-provoking, some just fun. The goal isn't to tell you what to think — it's to put honest, well-sourced numbers under the questions, so you can decide for yourself. Sources always cited. Methodology always shown.

What this is

A one-person data project run under the name @unwelcomedata. Everything here starts with a question worth being curious about, gets built from public data, and gets published in full — the chart, the cleaned data, and the sources behind it.

  • Public data only, with full attribution and retrieval dates.
  • Methodology and caveats stated plainly — including where the data is weak.
  • Charts describe what the data shows; they don't tell you what to conclude.
  • Serious topics and fun ones, side by side, on purpose.
